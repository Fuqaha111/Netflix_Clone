Dexter: Resurrection

Dexter: Resurrection is a Paramount+ with Showtime crime drama thriller series that serves as a direct sequel to Dexter: New Blood. Miraculously surviving a gunshot wound from his son Harrison due to freezing winter temperatures, Dexter Morgan (Michael C. Hall) awakens from a 10-week coma. Upon learning that Harrison has fled to New York City and accidentally committed a copycat murder of his own, Dexter heads to the Big Apple to protect his son and cover his tracks. Operating under the radar, Dexter soon finds himself infiltrating a macabre secret society of active serial killers curated by a wealthy eccentric billionaire named Leon Prater (Peter Dinklage), all while his former Miami Metro colleague Angel Batista (David Zayas) aggressively pursues him to finally expose him as the Bay Harbor Butcher.
